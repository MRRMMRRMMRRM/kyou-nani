Thank you for using “What’s Today For?” Here are answers to common questions.

I want to draw again

You can draw one card a day. When the date changes, you can draw a new card.

My friend got a different prompt

The prompt is chosen at random when you draw a card, so different people get different prompts. You won’t see the same prompt again until you have drawn all of them once.

How do I write a note?

There are three ways:

  • On the Today screen, tap the card you drew. It flips over and opens the writing screen.
  • Tap the evening reminder.
  • Open the calendar with the button at the top left, then tap a day.

Your note is saved automatically.

The evening reminder doesn’t arrive

The reminder arrives once, in the evening, on days you draw a card. It does not arrive when:

  • You haven’t drawn a card that day
  • You finished writing your note before the reminder time
  • You drew the card after the reminder time

If it still doesn’t arrive, please check:

  • “Evening reminder” is on in Settings (the gear at the top right of the calendar)
  • Notifications are allowed in your iPhone’s Settings → Notifications → What’s Today
  • A Focus mode isn’t silencing notifications

I want to look back at past prompts and notes

Open the calendar with the button at the top left. Small cards appear on the days you drew one, and an ink dot marks the days you wrote a note. Tap a day to see its prompt and note.

My records don’t appear on my iPad or other devices

Records are shared through iCloud between iPhones and iPads signed in with the same Apple Account. Please check:

  • Both devices are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account
  • “What’s Today” is turned on in Settings → your name → iCloud, under the apps that use iCloud

Records can take a little while to arrive. Closing and reopening the App may help.

I got a new iPhone

Sign in to iCloud on the new device with the same Apple Account, then install the App. Your records will come back. This can take a little while.

Changing the App’s language

The App appears in Japanese when your device is set to Japanese, and in English otherwise. To change only the App’s language, go to Settings → Apps → What’s Today → Language.

Show today’s prompt on my Lock Screen

Use the Lock Screen widget.

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, then tap Customize.
  2. Choose Lock Screen, then tap the widget area below (or above) the clock.
  3. Choose What’s Today.

Before you draw, it shows “Draw today’s card.” After you draw, it shows today’s prompt. Your notes are never shown.

Post my card to X or other apps

On the Today screen, tap Share. The share sheet opens with an image of your card and the prompt. Choose where to send it. If X doesn’t appear in the share sheet, check that the X app is installed.
