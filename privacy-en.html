What’s Today For?

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Developer: まいら (MYRA)

The iPhone and iPad app “What’s Today For?” (Japanese title: 今日なにする日; “the App”) respects your privacy and works to protect your personal information.

Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or location. There is no account to create.

Information the App stores

The App stores the following:

  • The prompt on the card you draw, and when you drew it
  • The notes you write
  • Whether the evening reminder is on, and its time

Prompts, dates and notes are stored on your device and in your own iCloud (a service provided by Apple), so that you can see the same records on any iPhone or iPad signed in with the same Apple Account. Records stored in iCloud can only be read with your own Apple Account. The developer cannot see them.

Reminder settings, and a copy of today’s prompt for the Lock Screen widget, are stored only on your device.

Network access

  • To keep the list of prompts up to date, the App downloads a prompt file that the developer hosts on GitHub Pages (a service of GitHub, Inc.). The App does not send any information about you. As with any website, GitHub may record access information such as IP addresses.
  • Syncing with iCloud is handled by Apple, and Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Evening reminder

The evening reminder is scheduled and shown by your device. It is not sent from the developer’s servers. You can turn it off at any time in your device’s Settings.

Sharing to X and other apps

“Share” opens the system share sheet with an image of your card and the prompt. You decide where to send it, such as X (formerly Twitter), and whether to post or send anything; the App never posts or sends anything on its own. The card image is made on your device and never includes your note. Anything you post is covered by the privacy policy of the service you post to. If you save the image, it is saved to your device’s photo library.

Third parties, advertising and analytics

The App does not use advertising or analytics tools, and does not share your information with third parties.

If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in your device’s settings, crash reports from the App may reach the developer through Apple. They are used only to fix problems.

Deleting your records

  • Records on your device are deleted when you delete the App.
  • Records stored in iCloud can be deleted in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → What’s Today.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page.

Web version

The web version of the App (this website, in Japanese only) records the card you draw and its prompt only in your browser. These records disappear after about a week and are never sent to the developer. To display the pages, fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (a Google service). GitHub and Google may record access information such as IP addresses.
